What Terry can and can't do

Can

  • Tell you what you're scheduled for this week, by your first and last name.
  • Tell you what's on the church calendar for upcoming dates.
  • Tell you who's leading an event.

Can't

  • Change, add, or delete anything on the Onestop.
  • Tell you who's serving in future weeks. Only the current week's details are loaded.
